A heritage of performance

The Cloren heritage began in 1975 when Peter Cloeren, Sr.sketched the first Cloeren Feedblock.
Today, we still help you find the best solution to improve an existing extrusion process or bring
something completely new to the market. That's how we've always done business: working
closely with you from material selection to process optimization to help you develop leading- edge,
high-performance, quality extruded products.
